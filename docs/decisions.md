## Owner decisions

- **Scope:** project 5 of 15, for researcher and developer job applications, built a few at a time with no fixed date. Third batch, started 2026-11-03, with `event-driven-backtester`, `stat-arb-pairs` and `regime-portfolio-construction`.

- **Dependencies:** `equity-factor-pipeline` (done, M1 to M7) supplies the `eqpit` data layer through a git install. `backtest-overfitting-lab` owns trial-log contract v1.0 and the Deflated Sharpe code.

- **D-05 local model:** Llama 3.1 8B Instruct; the owner's Hugging Face access was granted 2026-10-31. Llama 4 isn't suitable: its cutoff is later (August 2024) and it is too large for this CPU-only PC.
- **D-08 Tiingo:** not approved. GDELT is the only news source.
- **P-14 Loughran-McDonald Master Dictionary (ruling 2026-11-03):** "Use it now". SRAF's page says the word lists are "free for use in academic research" and that commercial use needs a licence from the authors. The owner treats this project as personal research under those terms. The owner downloads `Loughran-McDonald_MasterDictionary_1993-2025.csv` from sraf.nd.edu and accepts the terms; agents do not download it. `lta data lm-dict --path` installs it under `DATA_ROOT/raw/lm/`. - **FinBERT CPU run (ruling 2026-11-03):** at most 8 torch threads. It pauses while the crypto recorder's ingest p99 is elevated: `scripts/recorder_guard.sh` reads `lob record status` every 5 minutes, stops FinBERT and corpus builds when any venue's `ingest_us_p99` exceeds 1,200 us on two readings in a row, and resumes them once every venue is under 1,000 us. The recorder's ops log puts its normal p99 under 1 ms.
